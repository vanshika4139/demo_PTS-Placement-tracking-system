Help & Support

Guides and answers for using the Placement Tracking System.

Getting Started

  • Organizations: Super Admins can create and manage partner organizations under the "Organization" tab.
  • Candidates: Add candidates with full training, education, and contact details under "Candidates".
  • Placements: Once a candidate's Employer Name is filled, they automatically appear under "Placements" with auto-generated follow-up checkpoints.
  • Tracking: Monitor overdue, upcoming, and completed follow-ups across all candidates.
  • Reports: View placement rates, sector-wise breakdowns, and organization performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I mark a candidate as placed?
Edit the candidate and fill in the Employer Name, Job Role, and Joining Date under the Placement section.

Q: How do follow-up checkpoints work?
Checkpoints (Month 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 12) are auto-created once a candidate has a joining date set. Update their status from the Placements or Tracking page.

Q: Who can create organizations?
Only Super Admin accounts can create and manage organizations.
